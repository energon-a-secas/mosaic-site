## The scene

Someone at a kitchen table with twenty holiday photos and fifteen minutes, or
someone making one meme fast. They are **judging photographs**, and that is what
decides the theme: the surround a photo sits against changes how bright it looks,
so a dark editor makes people under-brighten and a white one makes them
over-brighten. The chrome is light so the room and the screen agree; the canvas
surround is a neutral mid-grey so the photo is judged against nothing.

That is the argument for light here. It is not "easier on the eyes" in general,
which would be a preference. It is that this tool asks you to make colour
judgements, and a dark surround biases them.
